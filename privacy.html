Privacy Policy — FBviddl

This Privacy Policy explains how FBviddl ("we", "our", or "the service") collects, uses, and protects information when you use any tool on fbviddl.com — including the Facebook Video Downloader, Reel Downloader, Image Downloader, DP Downloader, and Follower Counter. By using FBviddl, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this policy.

FBviddl is designed with minimal data collection as a core principle. We do not require registration, do not request personal information, and do not sell or share user data with advertisers or third-party marketing services.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

FBviddl does not collect, store, or process:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or any other personally identifiable information
  • Your Facebook account credentials, login tokens, or session data
  • The videos, images, or profile pictures you download through this service
  • Your payment information (FBviddl is and always has been free)
  • Your precise location data

No account creation is required to use any tool on FBviddl. You are anonymous to us throughout every session.

2. Information We Process Temporarily

When you submit a Facebook URL to any FBviddl tool, that URL is sent to our backend infrastructure (hosted on Cloudflare Workers) for processing. The URL is used solely to fetch the requested media or data from Facebook's public-facing content delivery system and return the result to your browser. Once the result has been delivered, the submitted URL is not retained in any storage system, database, or log. We do not associate submitted URLs with any user identity or IP address in any persistent record.

3. Automatically Collected Technical Data

Like most web services, FBviddl's infrastructure may receive standard browser-level request data when you visit the site, including:

  • Your IP address (processed by Cloudflare for routing and DDoS protection, subject to Cloudflare's Privacy Policy)
  • Browser type and version
  • Device type and operating system
  • Referring URL (the page you came from)
  • Pages visited on fbviddl.com and approximate timestamps

This technical data is aggregated and anonymised. It is used to monitor site performance, detect abuse, and understand general usage patterns. It is not used to build individual user profiles and is not shared with third-party advertising networks.

4. Cookies

FBviddl uses only minimal, technically necessary cookies to ensure the site functions correctly — for example, to preserve your browser session state during a download. We do not use tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or cross-site tracking technologies. We do not use Google Analytics or any similar user-tracking analytics service on fbviddl.com.

You can disable or clear cookies at any time through your browser settings. Doing so will not prevent you from using any FBviddl tool.

5. No Media Storage

FBviddl does not store, cache, host, or redistribute any media file you download. Every video, image, reel, or profile picture retrieved through FBviddl is fetched directly from Facebook's public content delivery network (CDN) and streamed directly to your browser. No copy of that media file is written to any FBviddl server, database, or storage system at any point. Once your browser receives the download, the transaction is complete and no record of it remains on our side.

6. Third-Party Infrastructure

FBviddl uses the following third-party infrastructure providers to deliver the service:

  • Cloudflare Workers — Our backend processing runs on Cloudflare's serverless platform. Cloudflare handles network-level security, DDoS protection, and global routing. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.
  • Content Delivery Networks — Static assets (CSS, JavaScript, fonts) may be served through CDN providers for performance. These providers receive standard HTTP request headers but no personal data beyond what any web request contains.

We do not use any third-party advertising networks, social media tracking pixels, or data brokerage services on fbviddl.com.

7. Children's Privacy

FBviddl is a general-purpose web utility and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children. If you believe a child under 13 has submitted personal information through this site, please contact us and we will take immediate steps to remove it.

8. Data Security

All connections to fbviddl.com are secured using HTTPS (TLS encryption). This means data transmitted between your browser and our servers cannot be intercepted or read by a third party. Our backend infrastructure is hosted on enterprise-grade infrastructure with access controls, monitoring, and security policies in place. Because we collect minimal data to begin with, the risk surface for data exposure is inherently limited.

9. Your Rights

Because FBviddl does not collect or store personally identifiable information, there is nothing for us to delete, correct, or export on your behalf under data protection frameworks such as GDPR or CCPA. If you have cookies stored in your browser from fbviddl.com, you can delete them directly through your browser settings at any time.

If you have any concerns about privacy that this policy does not address, you can reach us directly through our Contact page.

10. Changes to This Policy

FBviddl may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the service, applicable laws, or industry best practices. When significant changes are made, the "Last Updated" date below will be revised. We recommend checking this page periodically. Continued use of FBviddl after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how FBviddl handles data, please use our Contact page to reach us. We respond to all legitimate privacy enquiries promptly.

You may also find our Terms of Service relevant for understanding the full conditions under which FBviddl operates.
