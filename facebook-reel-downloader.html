How to Download a Facebook Reel — Step by Step

The process is the same on every device and browser:

  1. Open Facebook and find the public Reel you want to save.
  2. Tap the Share button on the Reel, then choose Copy Link.
  3. Come back to this page and paste the copied link into the input field above.
  4. Press the Download button and wait a few seconds while FBviddl processes the link.
  5. Select HD or SD quality and the file saves to your device.

The entire process takes under a minute. No Facebook login is required at any step.

Why Not Use Screen Recording to Save Facebook Reels?

Screen recording is a common workaround but it has significant drawbacks. It captures everything on your screen — including the Facebook interface, status bar, and any notification popups that appear during recording. The output is a re-compressed version of the video, not the original file, which means lower quality and larger file sizes. FBviddl downloads the actual source MP4 directly from Facebook's content delivery network, giving you the original file quality without any of those issues.

Facebook Save vs. FBviddl Download — What's the Difference?

Facebook's built-in "Save" feature bookmarks a Reel to your Saved Items inside the Facebook app. It does not create a file on your device. If Facebook removes the Reel, or you lose access to Facebook, the saved bookmark is gone. FBviddl creates an actual MP4 file stored on your device that exists independently of Facebook — you can watch it offline, transfer it to another device, or back it up permanently.

What Reels Can and Cannot Be Downloaded

FBviddl works with any Reel that is publicly accessible via a shareable link. This includes Reels from public profiles, public Pages, and public Groups. It does not work with Reels from private profiles, friends-only posts, or any content that requires a Facebook login to view. If a Reel has been deleted by the author, or if Facebook has restricted its distribution, FBviddl will not be able to retrieve it.

Always respect the original creator's rights. Downloading a Reel for personal offline viewing is generally acceptable, but re-uploading or using someone else's content commercially without permission is a copyright violation.
