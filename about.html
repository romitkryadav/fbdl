About FBviddl

FBviddl is a free online Facebook media downloader built for everyday users who want a fast, reliable, and privacy-respecting way to save publicly available Facebook content to their devices. The platform launched in April 2026 with a single goal: make downloading Facebook videos as simple as pasting a link — no software installation, no account creation, no unnecessary steps.

Since launch, FBviddl has expanded into a complete Facebook media toolkit. Today the platform offers five free tools: a Facebook Video Downloader, a Facebook Reel Downloader, a Facebook Image Downloader, a Facebook DP Downloader, and a Live Facebook Follower Counter. Every tool on the platform is free, browser-based, and works on Android, iPhone, and desktop without any app or extension.

What FBviddl Does

When you paste a public Facebook URL into any FBviddl tool, the platform processes the link through our backend infrastructure — hosted on Cloudflare Workers for speed and global availability — and extracts the media or data associated with that URL. For videos and reels, FBviddl returns direct download links for HD and SD quality options. For images and profile pictures, it returns the highest publicly available resolution. For the follower counter, it returns the current public follower count with a live-updating dashboard.

FBviddl does not store, host, or cache any downloaded media. Every file is pulled directly from Facebook's content delivery network to your device. No copy remains on FBviddl's servers after your session ends. All connections are protected by HTTPS encryption.

Our Principles

Privacy first. FBviddl does not require any personal information, Facebook credentials, or account access to use. You are never asked to sign up. We do not sell data, display intrusive advertising, or track individual users. See our full Privacy Policy for details.

Public content only. Every FBviddl tool exclusively processes content that is publicly accessible on Facebook without authentication. Private profiles, friends-only posts, and any content protected by Facebook's privacy settings cannot be accessed through FBviddl — by design.

Transparency. FBviddl is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or in any way connected to Facebook or Meta Platforms, Inc. We are an independent third-party tool that reads publicly available data. Our Terms of Service clearly outline what the platform can and cannot be used for.

Continuous improvement. We update FBviddl regularly in response to changes in Facebook's platform, user feedback, and performance data. Recent updates are published in the update log on the homepage.

Who Uses FBviddl

FBviddl is used by a wide range of people for legitimate, everyday purposes. Content creators save their own videos and reels for editing or archiving. Students and researchers save public videos and images for offline reference. Marketers and brand managers use the follower counter to monitor competitor Pages. Journalists and analysts download public content for documentation. And everyday users save Facebook videos to watch offline when they do not have reliable internet access.

FBviddl is intended for personal, non-commercial use. Users are responsible for complying with applicable copyright laws and Facebook's terms when saving and using downloaded content. We do not condone using this platform to infringe on intellectual property or to violate anyone's privacy.
