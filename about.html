About FbVidDl

Welcome to FbVidDl, a free Facebook Video Downloader made to help users download public Facebook videos and Reels online. We built this tool to keep the downloading process simple, without asking users to install any app or software.

With FbVidDl, you can download Facebook videos in available quality, including HD when the original upload supports it. Just paste the Facebook video link, and our tool will process it and generate download options within a few seconds.

FbVidDl only works with publicly available Facebook videos, Reels, Watch videos, and completed Live streams. Private or restricted videos cannot be processed because of Facebook's privacy settings.

We do not store or host any downloaded videos. We only process the Facebook video link and generate a temporary download link that is shown only on your device. After the download is completed, that generated link is discarded.

Our mission is to provide a simple, clean, and reliable Facebook video downloader that anyone can use without unnecessary steps. We are continuously improving FbVidDl to support more Facebook video formats and provide a better downloading experience.
