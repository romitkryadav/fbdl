Why Does Facebook Show Low-Quality Images When You Save Them?

Facebook applies compression to all uploaded photos and serves different resolution versions depending on how and where you access them. When you right-click and save an image directly from your browser, you typically get the display-optimised version — which is smaller and more compressed than the original. FBviddl retrieves images through the post's source data, targeting the highest resolution version that Facebook makes publicly available for that post.

Note that the quality of what FBviddl returns is limited by what Facebook stores publicly. If the original upload was low-resolution, the download will match that resolution. FBviddl cannot increase image quality beyond what the original uploader provided.

Can You Download Photos From a Facebook Album?

Yes. If you paste the link to a public Facebook album post, FBviddl will attempt to extract all images within that post. For large multi-image albums, you may need to use the individual photo URL (the URL shown in the browser when you click a specific photo) to reliably download each image at full resolution. This works for photos shared on public profiles, public Pages, and public Groups.

What Facebook Images Cannot Be Downloaded

FBviddl only works with publicly accessible content. It cannot retrieve images from private profiles, friends-only posts, locked posts, or any photo that requires a Facebook login to view. If you paste a link to a private post, FBviddl will return an error. This restriction is by design — FBviddl never logs into Facebook or accesses any account on your behalf.

Always respect copyright when using downloaded images. Content creators retain ownership of their photos even when those photos are publicly visible on Facebook. Do not re-publish or use someone else's photos commercially without obtaining permission first.
