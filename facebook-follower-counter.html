Importance of Facebook Follower Counts

A follower count serves as an easy measurement of an audience on Facebook. For creators, businesses, personalities, and marketers, increase or decrease of the follower count can help in assessing the performance of their pages. An increase in the number of followers can mean that the content is reaching more users, while a sudden fall may indicate cases of audience loss or changes in content strategy. The follower count must not be regarded as the only indicator of success. So, measures like engagement, reach, video views, comments, shares, and conversions can tell much more about how good the audience is than the follower count does. Consequently, the Facebook Follower Counter is of the most value only when integrated with other social media metrics.

Track Public Facebook Creators and Competitors

Checking multiple Facebook Pages manually can be a chore at times. When researching creators, brands, public Pages, or competitors, a follower tracker can help in alleviating the need to keep lots of screenshots or spreadsheets with the information sources on the audience growth. Your follower tracker makes it possible to take measurements of several accounts and measure their audience size over time. For instance, if a marketer wants to improve their knowledge about five competing Pages, they can track their public followers on a weekly basis and find out which Page has a faster growth rate. This information can form the basis for simple competitor research, but it is important to remember that follower count should not be the sole criterion for measuring the quality of a Page.
