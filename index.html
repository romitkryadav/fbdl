Download Facebook Videos Directly in Your Browser

FBviddl uses a simple process that takes place in your browser. Paste a link to any public Facebook video into the tool above to obtain a downloadable copy on your computer or mobile device. No downloads or installations on your device are required. The following sections explain how this tool works, what content you can download, and what to do if a download does not work.

What FBviddl Can and Cannot Download

This tool allows you to download videos that have been shared publicly — meaning anyone can access the post and watch the video without logging into Facebook. FBviddl can therefore download videos from public profiles and Pages.

FBviddl cannot download videos restricted to Friends Only, videos shared in closed or secret Groups, or videos hidden behind any other privacy settings. This is not a limitation anyone is trying to solve — FBviddl simply uses the same mechanism as a regular, logged-out visit to Facebook and cannot bypass privacy settings.

Downloading on Android and iPhone

You can download videos on your phone using a similar process to a computer, with one extra step. Open the video in the Facebook app, tap Share, and select Copy Link. Open your browser, visit FBviddl, and paste the copied link.

How the download is handled depends on the operating system. On iPhone with Safari, you may be prompted to save the video to Photos or Files — check both apps if you do not see a confirmation. On Android, downloaded videos are usually saved automatically to the Downloads folder, where you can find them in the gallery or file manager.

If the usual steps do not work, try visiting the video in the browser instead of the app and copy the URL directly from the address bar.

FBviddl on a Desktop Computer

On Windows, macOS, or Linux, the steps are the same: copy the URL from Facebook, paste it into FBviddl, and download the video from your browser's downloads folder. If your browser does not start the download automatically, right-click the link and choose "Save link as" to choose the download location.

What Video Quality to Expect

FBviddl retrieves the video directly from Facebook's servers — it does not upscale, re-encode, or improve the content. If the video was uploaded in low quality, it will be downloaded in low quality. If it was uploaded in HD, the download will reflect that. No tool can add quality that was not in the original upload. Be cautious of any downloader that claims to guarantee a specific resolution regardless of the original video.

Reasons a Facebook Video Download May Fail

  1. The video has privacy restrictions. If the video is set to private or requires login to view, FBviddl cannot access it.
  2. The link was copied from the app share sheet. Links from Facebook's mobile app share sheet may take time to resolve or may not work at all. Copy the link from a web browser instead.
  3. The video was made private or deleted after the link was shared. Once a video is made private or removed, the link stops working immediately.
  4. Everything looks correct but the download does not start. This can happen occasionally. Facebook regularly changes its platform, and there may be a temporary delay before the downloader catches up.
  5. The file downloads but will not play. The file may be incomplete or in a format your player does not support. Try a different media player or re-download the file.
