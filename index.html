Why Did My Facebook Video Fail to Download?

If a download doesn't go through, it's usually caused by one of the following:

  • The video is private or restricted to a specific audience.
  • The link you pasted is incorrect, incomplete, or formatted differently than expected.
  • The original post or video has been deleted or is no longer publicly available.
  • Facebook's servers are temporarily unavailable or slow to respond.
  • The specific video or link format isn't currently supported.
  • The service is temporarily unable to process the request.

If a download fails, double-check that the video is genuinely public and that you've copied the full, correct link, then try again.

Not every video offers the same set of quality options. Here's what the two general options mean.

Facebook Video Downloader vs Facebook's Built-in Save Feature

Facebook's own "Save" option is designed to bookmark a post or video so you can find it again later inside the Facebook app or website. It keeps the content within Facebook's ecosystem rather than putting a file on your device.

A downloader like FBviddl serves a different purpose: it saves an actual video file to your phone or computer so you can watch it offline or keep a local copy, separate from your Facebook account. One organises content inside Facebook; the other creates a file outside of it.
