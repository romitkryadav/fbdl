HD vs. SD — Which Quality Should You Choose?

When FBviddl processes a video, it presents two quality options when both are available: HD (High Definition) and SD (Standard Definition). HD delivers the highest available resolution — typically 720p or 1080p depending on what the original uploader posted. SD is a compressed version that is smaller in file size, which is useful when storage or data is limited. If the original video was uploaded in HD, the HD download will match that quality exactly. FBviddl does not re-encode or compress any video — what you download is what Facebook stores.
